About

Built around execution.

Who We Are

An Indian merchant export company

Dasverra Private Limited is an India-based merchant export company working with international buyers across product categories and sourcing requirements.

We do not start by pushing a catalogue. We start by understanding the requirement: product, specification, quantity, quality expectations, destination and commercial terms.

The company exists around a simple idea. International supply becomes difficult when every part of the process is handled separately: sourcing, quality, packaging, documentation and logistics each treated as someone else's problem. Dasverra coordinates the supply-side journey around the buyer's requirement, so that process is easier to manage from a single point of contact.

What We Do

The role Dasverra plays

Requirement Interpretation

Turning a buyer's need into a workable sourcing specification.

Supply Discovery

Identifying suitable supply capability for the requirement.

Supplier Coordination

Managing communication and execution with supply-side partners.

Commercial Structuring

Agreeing price, Incoterm, payment terms and timeline before execution.

Execution Coordination

Tracking production, specification and packaging through to completion.

Export Documentation & Shipment

Coordinating the paperwork and freight movement for the order.

Dasverra coordinates the transaction. Manufacturing, inspection, logistics and other specialist activities may be handled by appropriate external partners depending on the requirement.

The Company

Built to make cross-border trade predictable

Dasverra Private Limited was incorporated in 2025 and is registered in Cuttack, Odisha.

We started by listening to both sides of the trade. Suppliers worried about getting paid. Buyers worried about quality and consistency. Intermediaries added confusion, and many deals never repeated. The same problems showed up across commodities, so we built the structure around the trade: verified parties, clear terms and one accountable coordinator.

Our first corridor supplied cotton fabrics from Indian mills to garment makers in Nigeria. Today we work across food and agricultural products, coconut, cashew, textiles, industrial supply and minerals.
