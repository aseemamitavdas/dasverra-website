Where does it stand?

Enter the reference we gave you, such as DV-B-261010-2FH3. You see the current stage and the last update and, once cargo is moving, its route, dates and progress. Names, prices and the documents themselves are never shown here.

Check a reference

Can’t find your reference? It starts with DV and is shown when you submit a form. Write to aseem.das@dasverra.org or message us on WhatsApp and we will look it up.

What the stages mean
  1. 1
    ReceivedWe have your application and logged it under this reference.
  2. 2
    Under reviewOur trade desk is reviewing your details.
  3. 3
    VerificationCompany details and references are being checked.
  4. 4
    Introduction callA short call to confirm the requirement, capacity or lanes.
  5. 5
    OnboardedYou are on the Dasverra network and considered for live requirements.
  1. 1
    RequirementThe requirement is logged with product, specification, quantity and destination.
  2. 2
    FeasibilityWe are checking supply capability, minimum order quantity, lead time and commercial feasibility.
  3. 3
    ConfirmationSamples, specifications and packaging are being aligned with the buyer.
  4. 4
    CommercialPrice, Incoterm and payment terms are being agreed.
  5. 5
    PaymentThe order is confirmed and the agreed payment step is under way.
  6. 6
    ExecutionProduction, packing and specification checks are being tracked.
  7. 7
    VerificationInspection and quality checks are being carried out as agreed.
  8. 8
    DocumentationShipping and export documents are being prepared.
  9. 9
    ShippingThe cargo is moving under the agreed Incoterm.
  10. 10
    DeliveryHandover, document sharing and commercial closure.
  1. 1
    BookedSpace is booked with the carrier and the booking details are confirmed.
  2. 2
    Cargo readyThe cargo is packed, checked and ready to move to the port or airport.
  3. 3
    Cleared at originExport clearance is done and the cargo is handed to the carrier.
  4. 4
    In transitThe cargo is on its way. The expected arrival is updated if the schedule changes.
  5. 5
    ArrivedThe cargo has reached the destination port or airport.
  6. 6
    Cleared at destinationImport clearance is done and the cargo can be released.
  7. 7
    Out for deliveryThe cargo is being moved to the final delivery point.
  8. 8
    DeliveredThe cargo has been handed over. Documents are shared as agreed.
