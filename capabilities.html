Capabilities

What we handle.

From sourcing to shipment, Dasverra coordinates the commercial and execution requirements around the transaction. Not every order uses every capability below.

1. Sourcing

Finding suitable supply based on the buyer's requirement.

2. Supplier Coordination

Managing communication and execution between buyer-side requirements and supply-side partners.

3. Product Development

Supporting specifications, samples and product adaptation where required.

4. Production Coordination

Following production against agreed specifications and timelines.

5. Quality & Inspection

Supporting buyer, third-party or agreed verification processes.

6. Packaging

Coordinating export-appropriate packaging based on product and shipment requirements.

7. Documentation

Coordinating commercial and export documentation as applicable.

8. Logistics Coordination

Working with relevant logistics and freight partners according to the agreed shipment structure.

9. Export Execution

Bringing the commercial, operational and documentation elements together for shipment.

10. Warehousing & Distribution

Storage and onward distribution in India through partner warehouses, for wholesale and B2B buyers.

The exact scope depends on the requirement and agreed transaction structure.

How capabilities are delivered

One accountable coordinator. Specialist partners where needed.

Dasverra coordinates the transaction. Manufacturing, inspection, logistics and other specialist activities may be handled by appropriate external partners, depending on the requirement.

The scope is agreed before execution, so everyone knows who does what.

Dasverra

Requirement interpretation, supplier identification and coordination, commercial structuring, documentation and shipment coordination.

Manufacturers & suppliers

Production to the agreed specification, samples, packing and readiness for dispatch.

Inspection agencies

Buyer, third-party or agreed verification, where the transaction requires it.

Freight forwarders

Booking, customs clearance and transport under the agreed Incoterm, with milestone updates.
