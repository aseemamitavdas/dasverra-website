Trade OS · Early access

A clearer way to trade with India.

Trade OS is Dasverra’s early-access toolkit for buyers and suppliers: a product catalogue, one combined RFQ, a live Trade Board and stage-by-stage tracking. There is no login. Our trade desk runs it behind the scenes.

The trade workflow

Ten stages, the same ones we work through.

Every trade follows the sequence on How We Work. The tracker shows the stage reached, so you always know what happens next and who is doing it.

  1. Requirement
  2. Feasibility
  3. Confirmation
  4. Commercial
  5. Payment
  6. Execution
  7. Verification
  8. Documentation
  9. Shipping
  10. Delivery

The tracker shows progress. It does not show prices, company names or documents, and it does not hold funds.

Stages, not guesses

Each update is a stage change recorded by the trade desk, with the date it happened.

Applications too

Every buyer, supplier, forwarder and partner application gets a reference and appears on the tracker as soon as we receive it.

Private by default

The board and the tracker never show company names, contact details, prices or documents.

People in the loop

Feasibility, matching and commercial terms are handled by the trade desk. Nothing is quoted or matched automatically.

Plain Language

What Trade OS is not

It is early access, and we would rather be clear about the limits than let anyone assume.

Not a self-service marketplace

There is no checkout and no instant quote. Price depends on the actual requirement, and terms are agreed with the trade desk for each trade.

Not an escrow or payment service

Trade OS shows where a trade stands. It does not hold or move money. Payment structures are explained on Trade & Payment.

Not a promise of availability

Catalogue and Trade Board entries are indications until a requirement is confirmed in writing. Not every requirement can be fulfilled.

Still being shaped

Features will change as we learn what buyers and suppliers actually need. If something is missing, tell us.

Next

Partner portal

A login for documents, inspection reports and payment milestones in one place is planned. Trade OS is the early-access version, available today.
