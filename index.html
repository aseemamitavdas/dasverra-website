Finding a supplier is only the beginning.

A supplier may be able to make the product. An international order also needs clear specifications, commercial alignment, quantity planning, sampling, quality expectations, production coordination, inspection, packaging, documentation, shipment planning, payment terms and Incoterms.

Dasverra brings these moving parts together around the buyer's requirement.

We work with appropriate Indian supply partners and manufacturers, and coordinate sourcing, specifications, sampling, production, quality, documentation, packaging and export execution according to what each order actually needs. Manufacturing, inspection and logistics may be handled by appropriate external partners depending on the requirement.

How we keep the transaction clear

Written terms, applied to every order.

Commercial structure depends on product, buyer profile, destination, order value and transaction terms. This is the sequence we keep visible throughout.

  1. 1
    RequirementDocumented in detail before anything else.
  2. 2
    SupplierSupply capability evaluated against the requirement.
  3. 3
    SpecificationSpecifications aligned before production begins.
  4. 4
    VerificationBuyer, third-party or coordinated inspection where required.
  5. 5
    DocumentationExport documents coordinated as applicable.
  6. 6
    ShipmentShipment follows the agreed commercial structure.

Verification is built in wherever the transaction requires it. Quality is not guaranteed by default, and every step is subject to the agreed commercial terms.

Entity first

Every commitment is made and signed by Dasverra Private Limited, never by an individual.

Terms before execution

Price, Incoterm, payment structure and timeline are agreed in writing before production starts.

Specification first

Production proceeds only after specifications, samples and packaging are aligned.

Verification where required

Buyer, third-party or Dasverra-coordinated inspection, built in as the order needs it.

Documents before dispatch

Invoice, packing list, certificates and customs papers are checked before cargo is released.

Logged milestones

Collection, departure, arrival, clearance and delivery are recorded and shared as they happen.

Structured disputes

Direct resolution first, then mediation, then arbitration, as the contract sets out.

Review after every order

Feedback is reviewed with the buyer so the next order runs better.
