End-to-end services

Everything between the purchase order and the delivery receipt.

Use the full chain or only the steps you need. Every step follows the same control rules.

Sourcing & supplier qualification

We shortlist manufacturers on capability, capacity and compliance, and verify them before any order is placed.

Specifications & samples

Grades, specifications and packing are agreed in writing. Approved samples become the reference for production.

Commercial terms

Pricing, Incoterms (EXW, FOB, CFR, CIF) and payment milestones are fixed upfront and applied to every order.

Quality assurance & inspection

Production is monitored and inspected by a third party at handoff. Goods that fail inspection are not shipped.

Export documentation & compliance

Commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin and product certificates are prepared and checked before dispatch.

Freight & logistics

Booking, customs clearance and milestone tracking with SLA-based forwarders, from factory to port of discharge.

Warehousing & distribution in India

Storage and onward distribution through our partner network for wholesale and B2B buyers within India.

After-sales & disputes

Warranty claims are coordinated with the manufacturer. Disputes follow a set path: direct resolution, mediation, then arbitration.

The DasVerra Control Framework

Written rules, applied to every order.

Trade corridors start with personal relationships. They last when the rules are written down and applied to everyone, on every shipment.

Every step that can fail has an owner, a deadline and a fix.

Entity first

Every commitment is made and signed by Dasverra Private Limited, never by an individual.

Fixed payment terms

Standard terms apply to every order. Exceptions need written approval.

Inspection at handoff

Third-party QC signs off before goods pass from supplier to forwarder. Failed goods don't ship.

Documents before dispatch

Invoice, packing list, certificates and customs papers are verified before release.

Timestamped milestones

Collection, departure, port arrival, clearance and delivery are logged as they happen.

Two-step sign-off

Quantity is confirmed on delivery, and quality within 24 hours of receipt.

Structured disputes

Direct resolution first, then DasVerra mediation, then external arbitration.

Review after every order

Each completed order is reviewed with the buyer so the next one runs better.

About DasVerra

Built to make cross-border trade predictable.

Dasverra Private Limited is an India-based sourcing, procurement and export company, incorporated in 2025 and registered in Cuttack, Odisha.

We started by listening to both sides of the trade. Suppliers worried about getting paid. Buyers worried about quality and consistency. Brokers added confusion, and most deals never repeated. The same problems showed up in every commodity, so we built the structure around the trade: verified parties, written rules and one accountable coordinator.

Our first corridor supplied cotton fabrics from Indian mills to garment makers in Nigeria. Today we work across food commodities, textiles, industrial supply and minerals.
